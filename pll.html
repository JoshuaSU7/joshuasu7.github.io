PLL Feedback Divider

Designing the feedback divider inside a phase-locked loop at transistor level — one module of a full PLL being built by the SiliconJackets analog mixed-signal team.

Cadence Virtuoso Analog / Mixed-Signal ASIC

Overview

As part of the analog mixed-signal design team in SiliconJackets, we build modules for ASICs. One of them is a phase-locked loop. PLLs matter because they do the unglamorous but essential work of clock multiplication, signal synchronization, and cleaning noise off a clock.

My project is the feedback divider — the block in the return path that divides the VCO output back down before it reaches the phase detector, which is what sets the ratio between the reference clock and the final output frequency. It will be integrated with the components my teammates are designing to form the complete PLL.

Progress

Currently working through the dual modulus prescaler topology and implementing it in Cadence Virtuoso. A dual modulus prescaler can switch between two division ratios on the fly, which is what makes fine frequency resolution possible without running the whole divider chain at full speed.
