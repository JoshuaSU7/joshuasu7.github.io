Hardware Engineer

GT Yellow Jacket Space Program · Avionics — PCB Design, Altium Designer
  • Designed a 6-channel avionics sensor interface board in Altium Designer, implementing a 24 V input protection stage (fuse, TVS diode, bulk capacitance) and a buck/LDO power tree delivering regulated 5 V and 3.3 V rails.
